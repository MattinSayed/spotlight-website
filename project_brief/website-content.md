# SPOTLIGHT Dance Film Festival — Website Content

## Project Summary

SPOTLIGHT Dance Film Festival is an independent, curated dance film festival based in Frankfurt am Main, Germany.

The festival connects dance, film, music, visual culture, and community by bringing movement-based visual storytelling into a shared cinema space. SPOTLIGHT creates a platform for local and international artists whose work combines movement, image, sound, rhythm, emotion, and cultural expression.

The first edition took place in March 2026 in Frankfurt am Main and was completely sold out. The next edition is planned for spring 2027 as a larger one-day cinema event with curated film blocks, hosting, interviews, awards, photo and video documentation, and stronger partnerships.

The website should present SPOTLIGHT as an artistic, professional, and credible festival project for audiences, artists, cinemas, sponsors, funding bodies, press, and cultural partners.

# 2. About SPOTLIGHT

## Purpose

This section should explain the artistic and cultural idea behind the festival. It should answer: Why does SPOTLIGHT exist? Why dance film? Why a cinema setting?

## Header

About SPOTLIGHT

## Text

SPOTLIGHT Dance Film Festival is an independent, curated film festival based in Frankfurt am Main, Germany.

We created SPOTLIGHT to bring dance film and movement-based visual storytelling to the big screen, where these works can be seen, felt, and fully appreciated in a shared cinema setting. Many dance films live online or circulate within smaller creative communities. SPOTLIGHT gives them a focused space where movement, music, visuals, atmosphere, and emotion can unfold with full attention.

The festival brings together people from different creative worlds: dancers, filmmakers, choreographers, musicians, visual artists, and audiences who are curious about new forms of storytelling. It is a meeting point where creative disciplines overlap, people connect, and new ideas can grow.

At the same time, SPOTLIGHT highlights dance as an art form in its own right. Dance can tell stories, express identity, create atmosphere, and communicate what words sometimes cannot. Through film, this becomes even more powerful: movement, camera, sound, editing, and space come together to create a unique cinematic experience.

Our goal is to encourage artists to create, share their work, and experience dance film as a cinematic art form.

# 3. 2026 Edition

## Purpose

This section should prove that SPOTLIGHT already happened successfully. It should build trust with partners, sponsors, cinemas, press, and future participants.

## Header

First Edition — March 2026

## Text

The first edition of SPOTLIGHT took place in March 2026 at MalSehn cinema in Frankfurt am Main. The festival presented a two-hour program of 17 international dance short films and brought together dancers, filmmakers, artists, international guests, and a local creative audience.

More than 30 films from five continents were submitted. From these submissions, 17 films were selected for the final program.

The event was completely sold out with 80 visitors and an additional waiting list, showing a strong demand for a dedicated dance film platform in Frankfurt.

The first edition demonstrated that SPOTLIGHT has the potential to grow into a recurring cultural platform for film, movement, community, and interdisciplinary artistic exchange.

Vision 2027

## Text

For the next edition, SPOTLIGHT is planned as a larger one-day cinema event in Frankfurt am Main in spring 2027.
